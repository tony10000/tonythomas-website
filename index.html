About

I’m a writer, music producer, content creator, and technologist. My career has taken me through publishing, music and audio production, advertising, and broadcasting. Across those fields, I’ve stayed close to the work of communicating ideas and helping people connect with them.

Writing and publishing

My articles have appeared in Mix, Electronic Musician, Recording, Recording Engineer/Producer, AV/Video, Roland Users Group, Millimeter, Cleveland Scene, and other publications. I currently publish writing online through Medium, LinkedIn, Reddit, and other platforms. I’ve also written books available through Amazon, Apple Books, and other retailers.

Music and audio

I have decades of experience as a music producer, mixer, and audio engineer.

Advertising

I’ve held executive and creative roles at Rogers, Thomas, Cross & Long in Cleveland; Media One Advertising in Los Angeles and San Francisco; Target Communications International in Los Angeles; and The Thomas Group in Cleveland. Accounts included McDonald’s, Grumman Flxible, Greater Cleveland RTA, World Airways, Plantronics, SP Communications (Sprint), WDOK, Norstan, Roland, and Kawai America.

Broadcasting

I’ve been on air at WDOK, WERE, WQAL (Q104), and WRUW in Cleveland, and KBRT and KTYM in Los Angeles. I was also part of early internet radio, appearing on DAER in the late 1990s and early 2000s and later working with Legato Cafe.
